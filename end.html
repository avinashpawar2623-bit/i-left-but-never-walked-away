Thank You For Reading

This wasn't written...

To make you cry.

It wasn't written...

To make you come back.

It was written...

Because you became

A part of my life

That words

Could never replace.

If someday

You read this...

I only hope

You smile.

Because no matter

Where life takes us...

A part of me

Will always be grateful

That once,

I got the chance

To love you.

Thank you...

For being

The most beautiful chapter

Of my life.


❤️ The End ❤️

Some stories never truly end.

They simply become memories that stay with us forever.

One Last Message

Good night.

Love you...

More than you.

Sleep well.

Sweet dreams, just like you.

And one last thing...

Please don't change your decision for my sake.

Stay true to the decision you made for yourself.

Keep believing in yourself.

Keep growing.

Become the person you dream of becoming, not because I wanted you to, but because it's the future you chose for yourself.

I know you can do it.

No matter what happens, I'll always be proud of you.

Take care of yourself. Always.
